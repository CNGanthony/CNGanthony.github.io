About Us

Building brighter futures, one book at a time

Providing Pages Inc. is a fully registered and operational 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to distributing books and educational resources to communities in need. All paperwork has been filed legally, and we operate in full compliance with state and federal regulations.

We are organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes, including the distribution of creative works and media to support youth development, educational programs, and cultural enrichment initiatives. We collaborate with schools, libraries, and community centers to ensure learning opportunities are accessible to everyone.

501(c)(3) Registered Volunteer-Powered Community-Focused
“
Providing Pages began with a simple belief: everyone is creative, and everyone has something worth writing down. As a writer myself, I've learned firsthand that writing is more than a skill — it's a way to cope, to ease stress, and to steady yourself when life gets heavy. Reading does the same: it calms the mind, sharpens it, and opens doors you didn't know were there. I started Providing Pages to share what I've lived — to help students discover how powerful, and how genuinely healthy, reading and writing can be. We're just getting started, and I'd love for you to be part of it.
Anthony Kazan Founder, Providing Pages Inc.

Tutoring That Gives Back

Learn with SAT Compass, support our mission

Providing Pages partners with SAT Compass, a tutoring service founded by Anthony Kazan offering structured SAT, ACT, and AP exam preparation in math, English, and science — available online and in person.

100% of tutoring profits go directly to Providing Pages to fund our book and education programs. When you book a session, you're investing in your own goals and in your community at the same time.

100% of profits fund Providing Pages programs

Please note: tutoring fees are payment for a service and are not tax-deductible. To make your support tax-deductible, donate directly to Providing Pages via Zelle — and when signing up for tutoring, note that you'd like your payment directed to the charity.
